About Studio Within by Andrea

Studio Within by Andrea is a personalised printing, engraving and creative gift business specialising in unique, customised products. What began as a passion for creativity has grown into a business helping customers turn their ideas, memories and special occasions into meaningful products.

We are a small, home-based business with a big heart, creating personalised clothing and gifts for your special moments. From birthdays and baby showers to weddings and "just because" surprises, we bring your ideas to life.

Our Story

Founded by Andrea, Studio Within began as a passion project to create unique, personalised gifts that bring joy to special occasions. Over time, we've grown to serve customers throughout South Africa, from individuals to small businesses, helping them create products that tell their stories.

Every product we create is made with care and attention to detail. We believe that personalisation makes things special—whether it's a custom t-shirt for a loved one, business cards for a new venture, or a photo puzzle for a cherished memory.
